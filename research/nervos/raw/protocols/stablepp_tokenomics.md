# Tokenomics

### What is $STB?

Stable++ is an over-collateralized stablecoin protocol within the RGB++ ecosystem. The protocol token it's called $STB.

### What is $STB's max supply?

$STB has a maximum supply of 21,000,000 tokens.

### Is $STB a governance token?

No. $STB is a protocol token within the RGB++ ecosystem. It does not provide direct voting power or governance capabilities.

### How can I earn $STB?

$STB can be earned in several ways:

1. **Phase 1 Staking**: Stake $Seal for 7 days to earn 2% of $STB. Participants must stake at least 500 $Seal.
2. **Phase 2 Staking**:
   * Stake $Seal for 30 days to earn 6% of $STB.
   * Stake $Seal for 60 days to earn 12% of $STB. Users must stake at least 500 $Seal to participate in these phases.
3. **Liquidity Incentives**: Participate in the RGB++/BTC/CKB ecosystems to receive airdrops totaling 30% of $STB.
4. **Stable++ Ecosystem Airdrop**: Engage with the Stable++ ecosystem to receive airdrops totaling 40% of $STB.

### What can I do with $STB?

Holders of $STB can use the token within the RGB++ ecosystem, potentially for activities such as trading, staking, or other utility functions as defined by future ecosystem developments.

### What is $STB's distribution schedule?

| **Distribution Phase**                        | **Details**                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Phase 1: 7-Day Staking**                    | 2% of $STB distributed to $Seal stakers.                                |
| **Phase 2: 30/60-Day Staking**                | 18% of $STB distributed: 6% for 30-day stakers, 12% for 60-day stakers. |
| **Phase 3: RGB++/BTC/CKB Ecosystems Airdrop** | 30% of $STB distributed through liquidity incentives.                   |
| **Phase 4: Stable++ Ecosystem Airdrop**       | 40% of $STB distributed through ecosystem incentives.                   |
| **Phase 5: Team Allocation**                  | 10% of $STB allocated to the team.                                      |
