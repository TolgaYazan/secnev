# Welcome

<figure><img src="https://1798157326-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTDrjUwcFcfiqhK4vVhCS%2Fuploads%2F9DDjRfuAneKyLGILsTbK%2F01%20welcome.png?alt=media&amp;token=b9d2e749-4bd5-493e-b45c-812251d0da03" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Welcome to Stable++, an RGB++ based, over-collateralized stablecoin protocol, using BTC and CKB as collateral while USD-pegged stablecoin RUSD is minted in return.

Additionally, the protocol is safeguarded by an innovative mechanism designed to ensure all liquidations are processed efficiently and effectively. This allows us to maintain a relatively lower Minimum Collateral Ratio (MCR) for each vault, providing users with more flexibility while still upholding the stability and security of the system.

Stable++ is decentralized, immutable, permission-less.

Should you have any more questions after browsing this documentation, join our community!

Please note that all numbers and ratios mentioned are currently undergoing a financial audit. There may come some adjustments before official launch. Stable++ is dedicated to providing the best financial services and experience to all users.
